# Health-Care-Analytics
End to end data Health Care project
## Project Overview
This project demonstrates a production-style data pipeline built on a deliberately messy healthcare dataset of 1,000 patient records. The raw data contains mixed date formats, text-encoded numbers, placeholder contact details, duplicate rows, and ~38% missing values across key columns.
The pipeline is split into three independent, well-documented scripts each representing a distinct skill area for a data science portfolio.

# Python Data Cleaning
- Input: healthcare_messy_data.csv is loaded using pandas
- Output: healthcare_cleaned.csv
## Problems Found & Fixed
### 1. Inconsistent casing, trailing spaces:
- Strip + title-cas2
### 2. Word values/Non numeric values ("forty"), 159 NaNs, out-of-range:
  - none numeric values that are in numeric columns were corrected
### 3. Missing values:
  - Filled with mode
  - Median imputation
### 4. 5 different visits date formats:
  - Custom multi-format parser into → ISO YYYY-MM-DD
### 5. Phone number are incosinstance:
    - Digit extraction, placeholder removal
### 6. Email adress invalid formats:
    - Blocklist + regex validation → NaN
### 7. Duplicates:
    - Duplicate rows were identified and dropped

# SQL Analysis
## Eight analytical queries are run and printed with full transcripts:
- Patient summary by condition - count, avg age, avg cholesterol, avg systolic BP
- Blood pressure by gender - avg systolic, diastolic, and cholesterol per gender group
- Medication frequency by condition - count + % share within each condition (window function)
- Yearly & monthly visit volume - visit trend over time
- High-risk patients - SBP ≥ 140 AND Cholesterol > 200, ordered by severity
- BP category distribution - breakdown of Normal/Elevated/High per condition
- Risk indicators by age group - % high cholesterol, avg BP, % medicated
- Unmedicated high-risk patients - patients who may need clinical intervention

# Key findings:
# 1. Chronic Conditions Dominate the Dataset
- Asthma, Diabetes, Heart Disease, and Hypertension account for the majority of recorded diagnoses, with Asthma representing the largest patient group.
## This suggests the healthcare environment is primarily focused on:
- Chronic disease treatment
- Long-term patient management
- Preventative cardiovascular monitoring rather than acute or emergency healthcare services.
# 2. Dataset Represents High-Frequency Monitoring Rather Than a Large Population
- The dataset contains 1,000 healthcare records generated by only 10 unique patients.
## Key observations:
- John Doe recorded the highest number of visits (118)
- Jane Smith recorded the fewest (83)
### This indicates the dataset reflects:
- Longitudinal patient monitoring
- Repeated follow-up visits
- Chronic disease management patterns
# 3. Patient Visit Volume Increased Sharply Between 2018 and 2019
Healthcare visits increased from:
- 210 visits in 2018 to 409 visits in 2019 This represents nearly a 95% increase in activity within one year.
## Possible explanations include:
- Expansion of healthcare services
- Improved digital record capture
- Increased patient enrollment
- Better operational reporting practices

# 4.Cardiovascular Risk Factors Extend Beyond Hypertension Patients
Patients with:
High blood pressure (≥140 mmHg) and High cholesterol (≥200 mg/dL) were found across all disease categories, not only among patients diagnosed with Hypertension.
## This may suggest:
- Undiagnosed cardiovascular disease
- Undertreated comorbiditie
- Elevated long-term cardiac risk in the broader patient population
The trend indicates that cardiovascular monitoring may be necessary across multiple patient groups.
